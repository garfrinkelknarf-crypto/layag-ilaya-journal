COURSE INFORMATIONWhat you will be able to do

By the end of the course you should be able to:

CLO1

Show mathematical and scientific skills and knowledge on the fundamental concepts of Physics, particularly mechanics including kinematics and dynamics, and special topics on Physics.

CLO2

Manifest skills in carrying out designated tasks needed for gaining knowledge in different areas of Physics.

CLO3

Manifest thinking and writing skills in accomplishing written outputs and virtual outputs from designated tasks assigned in different areas of Physics.
